Good to know

A few things guests often ask

Where exactly are you?

In Karmane village at the edge of Mollem National Park in South Goa, close to Dudhsagar Waterfall and the Bhagwan Mahaveer Wildlife Sanctuary. Far from the beach crowds, happily.

What is included in the room rate?

Breakfast and a plantation tour. Excursions cost extra, mainly for transport, entry fees and guiding.

What is an organic swimming pool?

A pool filled with natural water and kept clear without chlorine, set right inside the garden.

Can we visit just for the day?

Of course. We host spice tours, day outs and camping as well as farmstays. Call or message us and we will plan it together.

How do we join the Feni Trail?

It is by invitation and kept to a small group. Message Moving Arts Program on WhatsApp and they will tell you about the next one.

Letters from the farm

Harvest news, new feni batches and Feni Trail dates, a few times a year. We are far too busy farming to send more.
