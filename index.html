Hello, and welcome

Come as you are. We will put the kettle on.

Dudhsagar Plantation is our family's farm: fifty green acres at the edge of Mollem National Park, a short drive from Dudhsagar Falls.

There are five cottages, a spice garden, a forest trail and a swimming pool filled with natural water. Mostly, though, there is time. Time for long breakfasts, slow walks and conversations that wander off somewhere nice. Stay a night or stay a week. Either way, you are very welcome here.
