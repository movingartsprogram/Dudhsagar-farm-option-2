Our story

It began with bare land and a stubborn dream

Four decades of patience, in one farm.

This was never meant to be a homestay. It was meant to be a farm that could look after itself. The guests came later, and we are so glad they did.

1984

No water, no power, no trees

Ajit Malkarnekar and his wife Doris began with barren land and one idea: bring the soil back to life, patiently and sustainably.

The next generation

Ashok comes home

Their son Ashok studied environmental economics in Germany, then came back to carry the same philosophy forward.

Today

Fifty acres that feed themselves

Fruit, spices, medicinal plants and cashew all grow here now, and the cashew becomes feni in our own still.

Gold for Sustainable Leadership, HomestaysOutlook Traveller Indian Responsible Tourism Awards 2025. We like to think the trees earned it.
