The Feni Trail

One day, one night, one farm in hidden South Goa.

By invitation, small group

From the tree to the still to the glass

Nothing for you to organise. Lunch, the walk, live music, the barbecue and breakfast are all taken care of.

Sunday 29 to Monday 30 November

Ask for an inviteSee the full trail
  1. Sunday, midday

    Lunch on the farm

    Everyone starts together, at one table.

  2. Afternoon

    Find the river

    Follow the trail down through the green to a small hidden river.

  3. Afternoon

    Where Ouro comes from

    The spice trail and the copper still where feni is made over a wood fire.

  4. Late afternoon

    The natural pool

    A swim in the middle of the green.

  5. Sunset

    Elvis Lobo, unplugged

    An acoustic set under the open sky, just for the group.

  6. Evening

    Barbecue and feni cocktails

    Fire, music and Ouro in a glass.

  7. Night

    Scorpions, then the moon

    A night walk to meet our glowing scorpions, then a long look at the moon.

  8. Monday morning

    Breakfast, then home

    Wake up on the farm and leave after breakfast.

From our own still

Ouro, our spiced cashew feni

Grown here, distilled here and best enjoyed here. Our heritage distillery makes cashew feni and urak the traditional way, and Ouro is the one we are proudest of. Take a bottle home from the farm, or find it in select stores across Goa.
